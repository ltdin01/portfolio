02 StockPulse A pricing engine that notices before you do. BEAIDAUI Java 21 · Spring Boot 3 · SSE 2026

The problemInventory distortion — too much of what doesn’t sell, too little of what does — costs retail about $1.1 trillion, and most repricing still reacts days late.

An event-driven decision engine built with Domain-Driven Design. Orders are written in under 10 ms; the thinking happens afterwards, off the request path. When demand velocity runs past twice its category benchmark, or stock breaches its floor, Gemini 2.5 Flash weighs margin elasticity against stockout runway and proposes a price and a replenishment batch — which is then clamped to sane bounds before anything ships. A pluggable Strategy Pattern swaps AI_POWERED for RULE_BASED, and a Time-Machine lab compresses a day into a minute so you can watch it work.

04 Competitor Spy Reading the competition so you don’t have to. DAAIBEUI SentenceTransformers · FastAPI · Recharts 2026

The problemCreators guess what to make next. The answer is usually sitting in what everyone else has already made.

About

परिचय

I’m Jay — a final-year AI & ML student in Mumbai who likes the unglamorous half of intelligent systems: the guardrails, the caches, the fallbacks, the review screens.

Models are getting very good at answering. I’m interested in what makes those answers safe to act on — and fast enough that nobody notices the machinery. Most of my projects end up the same shape: something quick at the front, something careful behind it, and a person somewhere in the loop.

I also can’t leave an interface alone. That’s how a WebGL gallery got its camera choreography, and how a small fix for Reddit turned into a browser extension now on release 0.41.

Notice to passengers

Standing rules I build by

  1. Fast path, slow path. Keep the request quick; do the thinking off it.
  2. Clamp the model. Every AI output gets bounds, a fallback, or a reviewer.
  3. Choose the retrieval. Vectors and graphs answer different questions.
  4. Never compute twice. Cache what the system has already learned.
  5. Accessible by default. Not a feature, not a final sprint.
